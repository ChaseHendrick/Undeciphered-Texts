## Proposed next experiment, not executed here

1. Introduce a new model only with a stated construction rationale and forward transform. Freeze the clue coordinates from the source before fitting. Do not derive a keystream from a purported full answer.
2. Reserve one disclosed phrase and fit the other. For an initial trial, cap the whole new portfolio at 10,000 model checks, period or width at 32, and saved witnesses at 20. Record the precise check unit and all eligible models; a check cap is not a coverage claim.
3. Validate fresh synthetic keys without passing the selected key to inference. Evaluate the reserved phrase after generation, count unknown letters separately, and disclose the number of alternatives surviving the fitted evidence.

A historical claim requires the complete text, method, key and exact forward correspondence, plus evidence independent of the fitted clues. An archival plaintext disclosure alone supplies a reference, not a demonstrated recovery method. More trials of the same underconstrained model do not create new evidence.
