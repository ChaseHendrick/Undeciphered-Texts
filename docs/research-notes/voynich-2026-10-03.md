## Corpus and access

The custodian's linked digital object is [Yale catalog 2002046](https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/2002046). That link was verified in Yale's page source; direct collection-viewer extraction returned 403 during this check. The manuscript images were not newly transcribed or downloaded in this pass. A catalog-access failure is not a missing-manuscript claim.

René Zandbergen's primary transliteration documentation distinguishes the visible-sign encoding from sounds or meaning. EVA, v101 and other schemes differ in their units and uncertain spaces. The current table links versioned IVTFF resources; the inspected [ZL file](https://www.voynich.nu/data/ZL3b-n.txt) declares version 3b dated 13 May 2025 and IVTFF 2.0. An automated combined reference is not an independent second human reading. [Transliteration methods and files](https://www.voynich.nu/transcr.html).

## Proposed next experiment, not executed here

1. Freeze ZL 3b and a second independently authored transliteration, including byte hashes and their own format declarations. Preserve folio/locus IDs, alternatives, uncertain spaces, missing text and drawing intrusions. Keep the sign tokenizer distinct from an ASCII text cleaner.
2. Preselect a bounded sample of at most 40 available folios and reserve 10 whole folios before computing features. Align only loci present in both versions, reporting excluded or disputed loci and the exact sampling seed. Do not assign sign values from plant illustrations.
3. Measure sign n-grams of orders 1 through 4, token lengths and line-position effects under each declared segmentation. Use at most 2,000 seeded null permutations per predeclared statistic, preserving word lengths for one null and line lengths for another. Report all statistics and multiplicity handling, not only the most favorable one.

The immediate output should be a disagreement table, sensitivity intervals and held-out predictive comparison. Repeated patterns or low prediction loss would characterize a representation; they would not establish a language, encoding or translation.

## Evidence required for a reading

Publish deterministic sign interpretation and decoding rules, preserve all exceptions, and predict untouched folios with the same rules. A proposed language needs independent linguistic grounding rather than a few selected word resemblances. If a held-out folio selects the rules, it is no longer independent validation. Bob's A-Z family model and English scoring corpus are outside this task's demonstrated domain; EVA's Roman-looking symbols do not make its strings English ciphertext.

The useful next trigger is a verifiable transcription correction, an independent corpus release or a reading that makes previously frozen predictions. These notes do not rank Voynich as close to a solution.
