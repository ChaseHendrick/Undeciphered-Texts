## Evidence required for a reading

Publish deterministic sign interpretation and decoding rules, preserve all exceptions, and predict untouched folios with the same rules. A proposed language needs independent linguistic grounding rather than a few selected word resemblances. If a held-out folio selects the rules, it is no longer independent validation. Bob's A-Z family model and English scoring corpus are outside this task's demonstrated domain; EVA's Roman-looking symbols do not make its strings English ciphertext.

The useful next trigger is a verifiable transcription correction, an independent corpus release or a reading that makes previously frozen predictions. These notes do not rank Voynich as close to a solution.
