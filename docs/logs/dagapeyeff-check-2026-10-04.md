# The rare column is not a check digit

4 October 2026. 1,320,000 predictor scores. No letter string is stored.

The thousand-attack scorer had a second copy of the tail count sitting after a return, so it never ran, and it rebuilt every score with fresh dictionaries. Both are fixed. The thousand scores are unchanged.

This pass asks whether the rare column is the sum, the copy, or the majority of the other cells in its row. Thirty-three predictors. The same search is allowed to pick its favorite on each of 20,000 shuffles of that column.

A planted column that really is the sum of the others scores 14 out of 14. The search notices.

On the real cells the best predictor gets 6 of the 14 row-digits right. All 20,000 shuffled columns also reach 6, so 6 is the floor of this search, not a fit. The column-digits inside those cells also top out at 6, and 15,059 of 20,000 shuffles reach that. The rare column is not a check digit of its row.
