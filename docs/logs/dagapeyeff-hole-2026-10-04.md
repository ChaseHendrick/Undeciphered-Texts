Two public writeups from 2026 were checked against the cells. Tim Marland's pipeline and a March note on MsgTrail both treat period 7 as a signal. On the 196 pairs, the repeat rate at lag 7 is matched by 1088 of 2000 shuffles. That is not under 5 percent. The lag was not chosen after looking at this grid.

The other check does not pick a column and does not slide a cutoff. The occupied frequencies are 1, 2, 3, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, and 20. The unique largest jump is 6, between 3 and 9. Every cell whose symbol is used 1, 2, or 3 times sits in column 14, the column named in 2014. Five of those eight cells were already on record, the ones used at most twice. The other three are the symbol used exactly three times. Given the five, those three sit in the nine seats the column still has in 4 of 54,435 placements. That is about 1 in 13,609, under 5 percent.

The next occupied count is 9. None of those nine cells sit in column 14. The high side of the hole does not follow the low side.
