remaining margin is not produced by the surplus homophone slots that
feed the climb. Within this instrument the real's statistics still
separate from nothing: they stand above homophonic encodings of the

instrument still cannot tell whether that shape identifies a cipher.
The ladder adds no exclusion, no identification and no closure; no
mapping is published, no assignment is stored,
plaintext_recovered_percent stays 0, and nothing here is a reading.

## Between the first generation and the frozen artifact

The artifact was generated once under the declared design (2:24), and
its first frozen form recorded each band member's five climb-half
totals only at the artifact's four-decimal rounding. The declared
selection reads exact climb-half totals, adoption strictly beyond
1e-12 and ties to the first draw, and one member (ZL latin, rung 33,
member 1) had five rounded totals coinciding at -4.5905 while an
exact difference of 1.8e-6 made its second draw's climb total the
best - so the stored artifact could not re-derive its own
selected index. The artifact was regenerated from the declared
module with each member additionally recording `draws_climb_exact`,
its five climb-half totals at full precision, declared in the
artifact's `band_record_rule`, and the regeneration verified: the top
rung's reproduction gate passed field for field again (421 fields),
and a scrub comparison of the first generation against the
regenerated artifact found every number and every other string
identical apart from the added disclosure key and rule string. The
measurement itself never changed.
