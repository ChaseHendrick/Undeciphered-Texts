# The glue is real. The order is not.

4 October 2026. 70,000 shuffles. No letter string is stored.

Each cell is two digits. The first digit is one stream, 196 long. The second digit is another. This pass does not call either stream a language.

Taken in order, neither stream is patterned. The row digits score 0.0275, and 15,767 of 20,000 shuffles of those digits are at least that predictable. The column digits score 0.0428, and 9,044 of 20,000 are at least that predictable. The best repeat-rate at any lag from 1 to 40 is ordinary too: 4,205 and 4,150 of 5,000 shuffles do as well or better.

The pairing is a different matter. A shuffle that keeps both digit totals and re-pairs them does not rebuild the cells. The score for that mismatch is 77.36, and 0 of 20,000 re-pairings match it. Cell 91 appears 12 times. The two totals, row 9 and column 1, account for about 3. Cell 61 appears 0 times. Those totals account for about 9. A textbook probability is not quoted, because a row that appears once makes some expected counts smaller than one. The shuffle is the test.

So the repeats live inside the cells. They do not live in the order of the cells, and they do not live in either digit read by itself. Renaming the cells cannot add order that is not there. Reordering them has already failed to reach prose. The thing that is actually uneven is which two digits share a cell.

No letter string is stored.
