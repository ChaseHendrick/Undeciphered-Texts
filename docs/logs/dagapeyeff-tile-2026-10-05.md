# Two blocks copy a square

5 October 2026. Not a reading. No letter string is stored.

Rows 12 and 13, columns 8 and 9, read 85, 84 over 75, 74. The next block, columns 9 and 10, reads 84, 75 over 74, 85. Both are the four cells 74, 75, 84 and 85, the 2 by 2 of the square on row digits 7 and 8 and column digits 4 and 5.

A block counts when its four cells are different and form a 2 by 2 on the square. The blocks may sit anywhere, and they may be two different squares. 743 of 20,000 shuffles have two or more.

One block anywhere is ordinary, about 25 in 100. A six-cell band filled from one such square is about 14 in 100. Neither of those is the claim. The claim is two blocks.
