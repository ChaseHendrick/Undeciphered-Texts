# Five symbols never leave the last column

4 October 2026. 20,000 shuffles. No letter string is stored.

The rare-cell pile was the cells that appear once or twice. That cutoff missed one symbol. Cell 92 appears three times, and all three times are in the last column too. So are both copies of 93, and the only copies of 04, 71, and 94.

| Cell | Times it appears | Column |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 92 | 3 | 13 |
| 93 | 2 | 13 |
| 04 | 1 | 13 |
| 71 | 1 | 13 |
| 94 | 1 | 13 |

That is 8 of the 14 cells in the last column. No other column owns a symbol outright. 0 of 20,000 random grids put this much private mass into one column.

Every one of those positions is 13 more than a multiple of 14, and therefore also 6 more than a multiple of 7. A period of 7 is the same column seen at half the resolution. It is not a separate 7-letter key. The other column that would fall on that coarser period, column 6, owns nothing.

A public note maps these five cells onto the codewords TT, BRIGADE, BATTERY, CK, and CH. That note does not recover a sentence, and this repo does not adopt the mapping. The measured fact is the column. The words are not.

No letter string is stored.
