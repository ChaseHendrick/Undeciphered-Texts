# The last-column symbols as padding or as rare letters, 7 October 2026

Not a reading. No letter string is stored.

Five symbols, cells 92, 93, 04, 71 and 94, occur only in the last column of the 14 by 14 grid. That is the last cell of each printed line. They fill 8 of the column's 14 cells, and 0 of 20,000 shuffles do that ([column](dagapeyeff-private-2026-10-04.md)). The fact is measured. What it means is not. `engine.dagapeyeff_rarecolumn` counts the two readings that can be tested from the digits and the corpora alone. The corpora are Latin (Thomas Aquinas and classical Perseus) and three English novels.

**Padding.** If the eight cells are nulls, the other 188 cells use 13 different symbols, so under a one-to-one key the message uses 13 letters.

No window uses as few letters as the padded reading needs. If the five symbols are padding, the rest is not plain Latin or English under a one-to-one key. It would need a key that gives some letters several symbols, another system, an unusual text, or no message.

**Rare letters.** The five symbols' counts, 3, 2, 1, 1 and 1, look ordinary. Many windows have five or more letters used at most three times: 2,623 of 6,184 in Aquinas, 958 of 1,448 in classical Latin, and 257 of 370 in the three novels. But real rare letters are spread through a text. In no window of any of these texts do all uses of the rare letters, at least eight, fall inside one stretch of 14 letters. A transposition that turned the last column into one contiguous run of plaintext would need that. A transposition that scatters the column instead would need eight rare letters to land in one printed column by chance, which the shuffles above put under 1 in 20,000.

**A habit at line ends** (by the maker or the printer) cannot be tested from the digits. Inspecting the 1939 printing is the evidence that could.

So the last column does not show that the cells are padding. Under every reading tested here it strains plain Latin and plain English under a one-to-one key. As padding, the rest uses too few letters. As real letters, they sit where letters in these texts never cluster.

Not a reading. No letter string is stored.
