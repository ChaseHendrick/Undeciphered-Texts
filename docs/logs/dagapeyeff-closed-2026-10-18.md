# D'Agapeyeff research closed for now, 18 October 2026

Not a reading. No search was run for this entry, and no result changed.

The repository owner has decided to close research on the D'Agapeyeff challenge for now. The reason is the owner's view that there is probably no recoverable message: a hand construction with no message fits every statistic measured so far, and every ordinary system that could carry a message under the cells' letter counts has been ruled out or needs chosen errors, an unusual text, or a transposition our searches cannot yet test.

That view is an interpretation, not a proof. The [no-message test](dagapeyeff-nomessage-2026-10-06.md) does not reject a dealing with no message (family-wise p 0.28), but it barely tells a turning-grille message from no message (3 of 20), and the [grille search](dagapeyeff-grillec-2026-10-06.md) cannot find a planted grille with the key unknown. A message under a 14 by 14 turning grille with a count-flattening key is therefore not excluded.

What stands as published:

- The ledger, [`dagapeyeff-status.json`](../research-notes/dagapeyeff-status.json), with 29 of 35 hypothesis families closed with shown power or excluded by a count and 6 left open. No family changed status with this decision.
- The preprint, *Excluding Classical Cipher Families for the 1939 D'Agapeyeff Challenge*, release 1.0.0 of 8 October 2026, in [`papers/dagapeyeff-exclusions`](../../papers/dagapeyeff-exclusions/), archived on Zenodo as [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23241304](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23241304). It is not peer reviewed.
- The frozen probes in `engine/` and their tests, which still run.

The ledger stage is now `closed`. The open families keep their planned next steps, so the work can be picked up where it stopped. If it reopens, the first job is still a grille search that recovers planted messages with the key unknown.
